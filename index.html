Shifts & Salary

Log your shifts and work out your pay under Israeli labor law, for anyone paid by the hour.

  • Shift calendar with Hebrew dates, Jewish holidays and Shabbat times
  • Overtime, Shabbat and holiday premiums, night hours and travel
  • Dashboard and a monthly report for Excel and PDF
  • Works offline; your data stays on your phone
